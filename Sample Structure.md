### Question 1: First Law of Thermodynamics
* **Question:** What does the First Law of Thermodynamics state about energy?
* **Options:** Energy can be created and destroyed, Energy is conserved and cannot be created or destroyed, Entropy always decreases, Heat flows from cold to hot
* **Answer:** Energy is conserved and cannot be created or destroyed
* **Explanation:** The first law is essentially the conservation of energy principle adapted for thermodynamic systems.
